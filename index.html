About Us

Hands-On Painting, Supervised by the Owner.

Rodas Painting & Services LLC is owned and personally run by Rogelio Rodas, who has spent 15+ years painting and remodeling homes and businesses across Falls Church, Annandale, Fairfax, and the rest of Northern Virginia. Rogelio shows up to give the estimate, and he's on site supervising the work — not just sending a crew and disappearing.

We paint with premium Benjamin Moore products and back it up with real carpentry, drywall, and remodeling skill — so if the wall needs patching or the trim needs replacing before it's painted, we handle that too. We're fully bilingual (English & Spanish), so communication is never the problem.

  • Bilingual — English & Spanish spoken fluently
